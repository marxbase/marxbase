# Data licence

The MarxBase data (the files in `releases/<version>/`: CSV, SQLite and JSON) are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may copy, share and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercial use, if you give appropriate credit:
cite MarxBase (see `CITATION.cff` and the website's Downloads page) and the original papers that the results come from.

**Exception.** If a release includes `evidence_sentence` columns, those sentences are quoted from paper abstracts. That text
belongs to the original authors and publishers and is not covered by this licence. Release 1.0 does not include quoted
evidence sentences.

The website code is separate and is released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).
